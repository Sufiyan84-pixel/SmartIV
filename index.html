Troubleshooting


App won't install
Enable "Unknown sources" again for your browser or file manager, then retry the install.


No data showing
Check your WiFi connection and confirm the Firebase database URL is correct.


Alerts not working
Enable the notification permission for Smart IV Monitor in system settings.


ESP32 not connecting
Verify the WiFi SSID and password flashed in the firmware.





FAQ
Quick answers to common questions




What is Smart IV Monitor?
An IoT system that tracks IV fluid level with an ESP32 and load cell, syncs it to Firebase and alerts nurses through an Android app.


Do I need the ESP32 hardware?
Yes, for live readings. Without it the app can only display demo or previously stored data.


Is it free and open source?
Yes — released under the MIT License. Use, modify and share it freely.


Does it work offline?
The ESP32 still runs its local OLED display and buzzer alerts offline. Cloud sync and push need internet.


How accurate is the fluid sensor?
Accuracy depends on load cell calibration. With proper calibration it tracks fluid weight closely, but verify it for your setup.


Can I use it in a real hospital?
It's a student/prototype-grade project. Real clinical use needs medical-device testing, validation and regulatory approval first.
